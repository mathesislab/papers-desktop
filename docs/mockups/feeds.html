Marine ice-sheet instability thresholds in a coupled ice–ocean model of Thwaites Glacier

Ortiz, M., Weber, L., Seroussi, H. (followed), Nakamura, K. · The Cryosphere 20, 6011–6030 · published 30 Oct 2026
We couple a higher-order ice-sheet model to a regional ocean model and map the tipping thresholds of Thwaites Glacier as a function of basal melt and sliding law. The grounding line retreats irreversibly once sub-shelf melt exceeds 32 m a⁻¹ in the reference configuration; the threshold shifts by a factor of two across sliding laws. Hysteresis experiments show that regrowth requires cooling well below present-day ocean temperatures. Our results suggest that the commitment to retreat depends more on the basal friction law than on the ocean forcing scenario …
