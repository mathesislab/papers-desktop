1 · The tab switch

Now: every tab grey until it is in front. Proposed: each tab keeps a light tint of its colour (Open orange, Library blue, Feeds green, Search violet), full colour in front. Variant B keeps the tabs neutral with a coloured underline. The PDF icon shows how many papers are open, dimmed with none.

2 · The Open tab, a PDF in front, details shown

Its own sidebar: the papers open (now the Open section of the Library's sidebar). Toolbar: the PDF's controls as now, then the three new ones (dashed): Details toggles the right pane (⌘⌥I), Show in Library (⌘⇧L) selects the paper in the Library tab, Cite ▾ copies (click: the citation in the CSL style; the menu: the rest). The Library tab is then the list alone, its sidebar without Open.
